FAQ

Have Questions?

How fast can you launch a team?
Our standard launch time is 14 days, from contract signing to your agents taking live tickets or making calls. For highly complex technical support, we may recommend a 21-day onboarding track to ensure absolute quality.
Is my data secure with your agents?
Absolutely. We are fully SOC2 and GDPR compliant. Agents access systems through hardened VPNs, work on restricted VDIs (Virtual Desktop Infrastructure), and our facilities have biometric access control. No customer data is ever stored locally.
Will my clients notice a heavy accent?
No. Unlike traditional offshore hubs in Asia, Armenians naturally possess a very clear, neutral English accent. Combined with our strict C1/C2 grammar testing and accent neutralization protocols, your customers will feel like they are talking to an in-house team in the US or Europe.
